## What it does

Share Project is an add-on (a "skill") for your AI assistant. You ask the assistant to share a project, it asks you a few questions, and it saves one zip file you can email. There are no commands to learn: you type the ordinary sentences shown in bold below.

The zip is a cleaned copy. Passwords and keys are left out. Real data files, such as customer or deal spreadsheets, are replaced with made-up rows that keep the same column names. Your own project folder is never changed.

## 1. Give the zip to your assistant

1. Open your project in an assistant that works on folders on your computer: the **Code** tab or **Cowork** in the Claude desktop app, or Codex.
2. Drag the share-project zip into the message box, or say where you saved it, and type **Set up this skill**.
3. The first time, it asks whether to keep the skill for next time. Say yes. After that you do not need the zip again.

Prefer to add it yourself? In the Claude desktop app, open **Settings**, then **Capabilities** (or **Skills**), and upload the zip, still zipped.

## 2. Share a project

1. Open your project the same way as in step 1 above, so the assistant can see the project folder.
2. Type **Share this project so someone can rebuild it**, or **Share this project so someone can help me fix a problem**.
3. Answer its questions, one at a time. It asks which folder, what the app does, and where to save the zip.
4. Read the summary it shows you: what is included, what was replaced, and what was left out. Say yes if it looks right.
5. It tells you where the zip is and gives you a short message to paste. Email both to the other person.

It will not work in a plain chat where you would have to upload your project. It stops and says so. That is on purpose: it checks the project before anything leaves your computer.

## 3. When someone sends you a zip

You do not need the skill installed to open a zip someone else made.

1. Save the zip and unzip it into a new, empty folder.
2. Open that folder with your AI assistant.
3. Open the file named `START-HERE.md`. It contains a ready-made message. Copy it and paste it to your assistant.
4. The assistant first tells you, in plain words, what it is about to do and what is missing. Answer its questions, then let it work.

A rebuild zip gives you your own working version; you bring your own data and accounts, and the zip lists what you need. A help zip ends with a reply note you send back. If you are the one who asked for help, give that note to your assistant and type **Apply this reply note**. It is advice, and you see what it suggests before anything changes.

## 4. Updates and problems

| When | Type this |
|---|---|
| Every month or so, or when Ben says there is a new version | Check for an update to the share project skill |
| Something did not work, or it worked and you want to say so | Send feedback about the share project skill |

Feedback is never sent for you. The assistant writes a short report with no project details in it, shows it to you, and you send it yourself.

## Good to know

- **Look before you send.** The summary in step 2 is your chance to catch anything you would rather keep private. The zip was checked, which is not a promise that nothing private is left.
- **You are among the first users.** If a step here does not match what you see on screen, tell Ben what you saw.
